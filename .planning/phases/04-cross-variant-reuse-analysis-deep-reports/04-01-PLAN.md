**Discovery:** Level 0. No new libraries. Extend the existing Python, SQLite, and pytest pipeline.

**Constraining decisions:**
- Compare one `assembly.normalized_reference` on the two variants. Do not group assemblies that only sound alike.
- Shared, left-only, and right-only counts use canonical component ids already on `bom_relationship`. Pending or rejected identity lines are unresolved and stay out of the ratio.
- Overlap ratio is shared divided by the union of those canonical component ids. Union of zero is ratio `0.0`. `high_overlap` is true when the ratio is at least `0.5`.
- Labels stay separate. One label per component row, in this priority: `unresolved`, `blocked`, `variant_specific`, `reuse_candidate`, `reused`, `left_only`, `right_only`.
- A Nordic `variant_specific` part is not a candidate and not unresolved. A similarity pair stays two components.
- `blocked` means a row from `blockers()` matches that component ref or the assembly ref. If the same canonical id is on both variants, it still counts as shared. The label is `blocked` so the conflict stays visible. Do not pick a voltage winner. Do not treat `MAT-10001` as `CTRL-AIR-01`.
- Do not edit `data/inputs/` or read `data/ground_truth/`. Do not change the return keys of `already_reused()` or `reusable_candidates()`.
</context>

<feature>
  <name>Assembly overlap for two variants</name>
  <files>app/services/analysis.py, tests/test_compare.py</files>
  <behavior>
    `compare_variants(conn, left_ref, right_ref)` returns a dict:

    `left_ref`, `right_ref`, `assemblies` (list sorted by `assembly_ref`).

    Each assembly dict has: `assembly_ref`, `canonical_assembly_id` (int or null), `shared_count`, `left_only_count`, `right_only_count`, `variant_specific_count`, `unresolved_count`, `conflict_count`, `candidate_count`, `overlap_ratio` (float rounded to 2 decimal places), `high_overlap` (bool), `components` (list sorted by `ref`).

    Each component dict has: `label`, `ref`, `canonical_id` (int or null), `side` (`both`, `left`, `right`), `source_bom_line_ids` (sorted), `anchor` (string of letters, digits, and hyphens only, starting with the two variant refs and the assembly ref), `records` (list of `{source_table, source_id, source_file, source_row}` from `plm_bom_line` joined to `source_file.file_name`).

    `variant_specific_count` is a subset of `left_only_count + right_only_count`, not an extra term in the ratio. `candidate_count` is the number of candidate pairs on that assembly, not the number of component rows.

    Cases, each on its own fixture after `build_canonical_model`:
    - Assembly `HVAC-M01` has canonical component A on `REGIO-STD` and `REGIO-NORDIC`, and canonical component B only on `REGIO-NORDIC`. Ratio is `0.5`, `high_overlap` is true, `shared_count` is 1, `right_only_count` is 1. A is `reused` / `both`. B is `right_only`.
    - The same assembly with only component A on both sides. Ratio is `1.0`.
    - Component B is the only part and it is on one side. Ratio is `0.0`, `high_overlap` is false.
    - A BOM line whose PLM source component has an identity reconciliation in `PENDING` is `unresolved`, `canonical_id` is null, and it is absent from `shared_count` and from the ratio denominator.
    - A component whose reconciliation `evidence_json.relationship` is `variant_specific`, present only on `REGIO-NORDIC`, is labeled `variant_specific`. It is not in `reusable_candidates` output used for this row, and `candidate_count` does not include it.
    - Two different canonical components on the same assembly, one on each variant, that `reusable_candidates()` already reports as a pair, are both `reuse_candidate`. `candidate_count` is 1. They are not merged into one `canonical_id`.
    - A component on both variants whose ref matches a `blockers()` row is `blocked`, still included in `shared_count`, and `conflict_count` is at least 1. Both blocker values remain on the blocker row; this function does not drop one.
    - An assembly reference that exists on only one of the two variants still appears. Assemblies on neither variant are omitted.
    - Swapping left and right swaps `left_only` and `right_only` and does not change `shared_count` or `overlap_ratio`.

    `all_variant_pairs(conn)` returns the unordered pairs of distinct `variant.normalized_reference` values, each as `(left, right)` with `left < right` lexicographically, sorted. Two variants produce one pair. It does not hardcode the five REGIO refs.

    Tests build their own rows. Do not require `data/processed/cognyx.db`. Follow the insert style in `tests/test_analysis.py`.
  </behavior>
  <implementation>
    Read `bom_relationship` joined to `assembly`, `variant`, and `component`. Read `plm_bom_line` for lines on those two variant refs and that assembly ref whose `id` is not a `source_bom_line_id` on `bom_relationship`; label those `unresolved`. Join `source_file` for `file_name` and copy `source_row`.

    Call `reusable_candidates(conn)` for candidate pairs and `blockers(conn)` for conflict matches. Match blocker `entity_ref` to the component normalized ref, the raw ref, or the assembly ref. Do not call `build_canonical_model` inside `compare_variants`. Do not insert reconciliation rows. Do not add an HTTP route.

    Anchor example shape: `REGIO-STD-REGIO-NORDIC-HVAC-M01-CTRL-AIR-01`. Replace any other character with a hyphen.
  </implementation>
</feature>
